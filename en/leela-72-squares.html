The 72 Squares of Leela

The ancient Vedic map of human consciousness. In the Game of Self-Knowledge (Jnana Chaupar / Snakes & Arrows), each of the 72 squares embodies an internal state of being, obstacle, or spiritual law across the eight chakra planes.
